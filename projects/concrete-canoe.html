Mini Concrete
Canoe Prototype

A scaled competition-canoe prototype and lightweight concrete mix reaching 909.71 PSI at 28 days.

STRUCTURAL DESIGN

OVERVIEW

A blueprint for the race canoe

In EGR101, our five-person team worked with the Duke chapter of the American Society of Civil Engineers (ASCE) as our client. ASCE needed a scaled-down prototype to guide the full-size, four-person canoe it would build and race in the ASCE Concrete Canoe Competition. Our mini canoe let the club test the concrete mix, reinforcement, and construction methods at small scale, catching problems before investing in the full build.

REQUIREMENTS

Float, survive, and look good doing it

The canoe had to be made entirely of concrete, stay afloat for at least two minutes when filled with water, fit within 5.5 × 2 × 3.5 ft, survive a four-hour trip to competition, and have no sharp edges. Beyond those constraints, we aimed for a length-to-width ratio between 4:1 and 6:1 for stability and speed, chips and cracks under ¼ in. after a year of use, and a hull a rower could sit in comfortably for about an hour.

DESIGN

Light, strong, and scalable

Our design had four main features:

  • Rounded hull bottom: moves through the water efficiently and is very durable.
  • Chicken-wire reinforcement: carries the tension that concrete alone can't.
  • No built-in seats: cuts weight while staying comfortable.
  • Lightweight concrete mix: designed to float, resist water, and hold up to racing.

To build the canoe, we packed the concrete by hand over a mold.

TESTING

909.71 PSI at 28 days

We cast test cubes from our mix and crushed them in a hydraulic press after 28 days of curing. They averaged a compressive strength of 909.71 PSI, typical of a strong lightweight concrete. A workability test showed how easily the mix could be shaped over the mold. We also ran buoyancy, slump, and ergonomics tests.

The next steps for ASCE are scaling the design to the full four-person canoe, testing it on the water at full size, and continuing to balance the mix between weight and durability.
