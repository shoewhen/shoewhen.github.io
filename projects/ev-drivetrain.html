BRAKE SYSTEM DESIGN

OVERVIEW

Eliminating brake drag

Member of the mechanical subteam at Duke Electric Vehicles. The current brakes drag: the disc contacts the pads even when the brakes are disengaged. I'm diagnosing the cause and redesigning the brake assembly to restore pad-to-disc clearance and minimize parasitic drag on the drivetrain.

DESIGN PROCESS

From diagnosis to redesign

This project is still in progress. Check back soon for details on the design iterations and the redesigned brake assembly!
