Sunflower
Shelling Machine

A human-operated, fully 3D-printed sheller that earned the second-highest performance score in a class of 30+ students.

EGR / 05MECHANICAL SYSTEM

OVERVIEW

Separate shell from kernel

Designed and fabricated a human-operated sunflower shelling machine. The system combines a gear-driven dual-roller compression mechanism with a 3D-printed kernel and shell separation path.

DESIGN & TESTING

Geometry informed by tests

Modeled assemblies in SolidWorks and built a removable sifting tray with an integrated collection chamber. Quantitative tests of seed geometry and crushing force informed channel spacing and roller cut dimensions, helping minimize kernel damage.

The design achieved the second-highest performance score in a class of more than 30 students.
