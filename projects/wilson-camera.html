Camera Mount

A modular, adjustable 3D-printed mount for a Raspberry Pi climbing-wall camera system.

PROTOTYPE SYSTEM

OVERVIEW

Mounting a camera on the wall

Developing a 3D-printed mounting system for Duke University's Wilson Gym climbing wall camera project. The mount needs to securely hold a Raspberry Pi, camera module, and supporting electronics while remaining durable, modular, and easy to install.

ENGINEERING

Adjustable by design

CAD work includes articulated hinge mechanisms, hardware mounting features, and custom-fit enclosures. Iterative prototypes support fit checks and future system integration.
