OVERVIEW

One machine, many handoffs

Built a multi-stage electromechanical Rube Goldberg course as part of a 15-person engineering team. Reliable timing and integration across autonomous machines were essential to the final performance.

MY CONTRIBUTION

The elevator return

Led the design and integration of an Arduino-controlled elevator that returned the ball to the start for a second run. Worked across subteams to coordinate sensors, motorized motion, and system timing.
